BOJ Bank of Japan Rate Hike — September 18, 2026

The BOJ voted 7-2 to raise the policy rate from 1.00% to 1.25% — the highest since 1995. This is the sixth rate hike since exiting negative rates in March 2024. Toichiro Asada and Ayano Sato dissented, arguing sub-2% inflation and limited economic acceleration did not justify a hike.
