Fed Raises Rates 25 bps to 3.75–4.00% — First Hike Since 2023
Sep 16, 2026

The FOMC raised the federal funds rate target range by 25 basis points to 3.75–4.00%, the first increase since 2023. The decision was approved 12–0 under new Chair Kevin Warsh. BOJ hikes to 31-year high, yen near 157, record intervention
BOJ Raises Policy Rate to 1.25% — 31-Year High
Sep 18, 2026

The BOJ raised its uncollateralized overnight call rate from ~1.0% to 1.25% on a 7–2 vote, effective September 24. Governor Ueda Kazuo and six members supported the increase; Asada Toichiro and Sato Ayano dissented. This marks the highest policy rate in 31 years.

The BOJ also raised the complementary deposit facility rate to 1.25% and the basic loan rate to 1.5%. It unanimously rewrote climate-lending terms, switching to a floating rate and capping each counterparty's borrowing at ¥10 trillion. The BOJ signaled it would "continue to raise the policy interest rate and adjust the degree of monetary accommodation."
